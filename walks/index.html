I am a person who likes to go on walks, whether it is with family or friends or alone. Normally I would just walk around my neighborhood when I'm bored, it is a good way to pass time and it also works out your legs, and for some people, it can be fun too.

Walks would be more fun if you're not alone in my opinion. You have someone to talk to which means if you have an interesting conversion going, you wouldn't even think about whether you are too tired to walk or whether you want to stop walking.

Benefits of Walking

Walking may not be considered as working out like hitting the gym, but it provides its own benefits. Talking walks from time to time can provide major physical, mental, and long-term health benefits, it provides things like lowering blood pressure, helps manage weight, strengthens bones, boosts immune systems, and builds endurance mostly targeting the lower-body muscles, taking walks also reduces anxiety, depression, and high cortisol.

Places nearby where I would take a walk

Title Data Description Trail map
Stanley Park Loop This place is about 2.6 miles long in length, about 65 ft tall on elevation gain, and takes about half-an-hour or to at least 1 hour depending on how fast you're walking, this place also goes in a loop. The Stanley Park Loop is a pretty long and chill area to walk in. The place has a Rose Garden, Asian garden and more making it more beautiful to walk around on. The place also features a colonial-era village which would be cool to see.
Columbia Greenway Rail Trail This place is about 4.5 miles long, has an elevation gain of 101ft, and it would take about 1.5 hours to about 2 hours. The area goes in a straight path. This trail is popular for road biking, running, and walking. It transitions from a scenic “town-and-country" landscape to a unique elevated urban corridor.
Westfield Wildlife Trail This area is about 0.7 miles in length, has an elevation gain of 183 ft, and takes around 0.5-1 hours to fully complete, most of the area is a line but there is a shorter loop at the end. This area serves as a critical habit for local conservation species. So there would be animals roaming around somewhere around here, these animals include white-tailed deer, black bear, eastern coyote, and squirrel. This place is also open for fishing, hunting and trapping as long as it is within Wildlife management zones 4s regulation.
Stanley Park This place is nearly 300 acres in total, it is at least 2.6 miles long, and takes 45 to 60 minutes to walk or run has an elevation gain of 148 feet. Stanley Park features an award-winning Rose Garden with over 2,500 rose bushes across more than 35 varieties, including a Colonial Duck Pond with a natural spring, cascading waterfalls, a working old mill with a water wheel, and more. You may also find animals like black and white geese or ducks, fish, turtles, and frogs
